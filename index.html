ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Welcome to PM SHRI GUSSS PARWANOO

PM SHRI Future Ready School

Education • Character • Excellence

PM SHRI Government Senior Secondary School Parwanoo, District Solan, Himachal Pradesh, is committed to providing quality education and holistic development opportunities to its students.

The school aims to develop confident, responsible, creative and capable learners through academic learning, co-curricular activities, sports, technology and values.

Message from the Principal

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome students, parents and visitors to PM SHRI GUSSS PARWANOO.
